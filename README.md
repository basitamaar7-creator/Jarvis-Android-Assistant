# 🤖 JARVIS - AI Voice Assistant System

**Complete AI-Powered Voice Assistant for Android, Web & Laptop with Cloud Sync, Social Media Control, Digital Marketing & E-Commerce Integration**

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎤 **Voice Assistant**
- Male & Female voice options
- Google Cloud Speech-to-Text
- Google Gemini AI integration
- Natural language processing
- Voice commands execution

### 📱 **Social Media Control**
- **WhatsApp** - Send messages, manage conversations
- **Facebook** - Create, edit, share posts + reply to comments
- **Instagram** - Create, edit, share posts + reply to comments
- **YouTube** - Upload videos, manage channel, auto-replies, automation
- **TikTok** - Post management & engagement

### 📚 **English Learning System**
- Grammar tutorials
- Vocabulary builder
- Conversation practice
- Real-time corrections

### 🌍 **News & Information**
- Worldwide news feed
- Real-time updates
- Personalized news based on interests

### 🎬 **Video Editing System**
- Video trimming & cutting
- Effects & filters
- Subtitle addition
- Format conversion
- Export options

### 📊 **Business Dashboard**
- Separate Business Mode
- Facebook Business Page management
- Instagram Business Account
- TikTok Business Account
- E-Commerce Website Integration
- Business Analytics & ROI tracking
- Client Management (CRM)
- Revenue tracking

### 📈 **Digital Marketing Suite**
- Analytics Dashboard
- Growth Tracking
- SEO Tools
- Social Media Growth automation
- Digital Marketing campaigns
- Email Marketing & automation
- Auto-Posting Schedule
- Content Ideas generator

### ☁️ **Cloud Sync & Multi-Device**
- Support for 3+ devices simultaneously
- Real-time Firebase Cloud Sync
- Same account across all devices
- Automatic backup

### 📧 **Multi-Email Support**
- Multiple email accounts per device
- Instant email switching
- Separate data per email

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

**Frontend**: Android (Kotlin/Java), React.js, Flutter Web
**Backend**: Firebase, Node.js, Express.js
**AI & Voice**: Google Cloud Speech-to-Text, Google Gemini API
**APIs**: Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Google Cloud
**Video**: FFmpeg, LibVLC
